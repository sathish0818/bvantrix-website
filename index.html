About RITVA

Responsible business
starts with understanding.

Every organisation has an impact on the environment, on people and on the communities around it. But do you know where your organisation stands today?

RITVA helps businesses understand their ESG maturity, identify the areas that need attention and take practical steps towards improvement.

Three dimensions. One clearer picture.

How it works

Your ESG journey,
made simple.

You do not need to be an ESG expert to get started. RITVA takes you through four simple steps.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your organisation

    Share basic information about your business, your industry and your current practices.

  2. 02

    Assess your ESG maturity

    Answer a set of structured questions covering environment, social and governance.

  3. 03

    Understand your results

    Get a clearer picture of your current maturity, your strengths and your improvement areas.

  4. 04

    Take action

    Explore recommended priorities and practical steps to improve your ESG performance.
